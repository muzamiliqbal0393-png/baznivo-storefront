Frequently asked

How long does delivery take?

2–3 working days for major cities, 3–4 working days elsewhere. Tracking is shared on WhatsApp once your order is confirmed.

Is cash on delivery available?

Yes — cash on delivery across Pakistan. JazzCash, EasyPaisa and bank transfer are accepted too.

What are the delivery charges?

Free above Rs 2,500. Below that, a flat Rs 200 applies.

How do returns work?

Message us within 7 days of delivery. Sealed, unused items can be returned; used items cannot. Return shipping is paid by the customer.

Are the products genuine?

Yes. We source only from trusted suppliers. If anything looks off, contact us with an unboxing video and we will resolve it.

Can you help me choose a product?

Of course. Send us a photo and tell us your skin type or preference — we will suggest the right option.
