Beauty jo aap ke budget mein ho

Baznivo ka idea simple tha: achhi quality ka makeup har ghar tak pahunche — na overpriced, na confusion. Hum sirf wohi products list karte hain jo hum khud test karte hain aur jo Pakistani skin tones par sach mein kaam karte hain.

Har order cash on delivery ke saath aata hai, poore Pakistan mein 2–4 din mein. Product pasand na aaye to 14 din ke andar return request kar sakte hain — koi lambi chakkar nahi.

Hum kya karte hain

  • Original makeup aur skincare — seedha trusted suppliers se.
  • Shade matching help WhatsApp par, insaan se — bot se nahi.
  • Safe packing, tracked delivery aur clear return policy.
  • Har product ke saath honest description aur ingredients.
