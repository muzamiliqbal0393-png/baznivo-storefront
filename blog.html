Beginners ke liye 5 makeup essentials

Bohat saare products ki zaroorat nahi. Ek clean, roz ke look ke liye ye 5 cheezein kaafi hain:

  • Foundation ya BB cream — base jo skin tone se match kare.
  • Concealer — dark circles aur chhote blemishes ke liye.
  • Kajal / eyeliner — aankhon ko define karne ke liye.
  • Lipstick — ek shade jo aap ke din ke look se match kare.
  • Compact powder — din bhar shine control ke liye.

Bas itna. Baqi sab baad mein — jab aap ko khud pata chal jaye ke aap ka look kya hai.

Long-wear lipstick: 6 ghante kaise chalayein

  • Honton ko pehle scrub aur moisturise karein — lipstick smooth baithti hai.
  • Lip liner se poore hont bharein, phir lipstick lagayein.
  • Ek tissue se daba kar halka powder lagayein, phir dobara ek layer.
  • Khane se pehle sirf centre saaf karein, poora nahi.

Brush care 101

Gande brushes na sirf product waste karte hain, balke skin par breakouts bhi la sakte hain. Har hafte halke shampoo se dho lein, bristles ko neeche rakh kar dry karein aur shape hold karein.
